01

Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy outlines the operational standards for Spot & Seek : Hidden Dude, an Android application engineered and distributed by Tiny Tricky Logics ("Company", "we", "us").

We respect the confidentiality of our players. This document transparently breaks down how technical telemetries, device signals, and diagnostic parameters are handled when you install and interact with our application.

02

Data Collected Automatically (Play Console Standards)

To ensure game stability, deliver advertising features, and monitor overall app performance, our integrated mobile frameworks collect non-personally identifiable technical indicators:

  • Device Identifiers & Technical Specs: Model name, hardware architecture, operating system build version, language preference, time zone, and Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Application Telemetry: Game launch timestamps, level completion durations, session frequency, and contextual interaction events.
  • Diagnostic & Error Reports: Stack traces, crash logs, and system state snapshots captured during unexpected application stops.
  • Coarse Network Location: Internet Protocol (IP) address utilized exclusively to estimate country-level region for ad delivery and security validation.
03

Purpose of Information Processing

Any telemetry processed through the application serves strictly defined technical and operational purposes:

  • Game Optimization: Adjusting graphics performance and gameplay balance across diverse Android hardware configurations.
  • In-App Advertising: Displaying non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad units via certified ad provider networks.
  • Bug Resolution: Diagnosing software anomalies and releasing stability patches via Google Play Store.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: Protecting the application ecosystem from automated abuse or unauthorized modifications.
04

Ad Services & Opt-Out Options

Our application uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to remain free to play. These networks utilize device Advertising IDs to manage ad frequency and deliver relevant content.

💡 Control Your Ad Settings: You can reset or limit personalized ad tracking on your Android device at any time by navigating to: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

App Permissions Required

To enable online gameplay sync, ad serving, and diagnostic reporting, Spot & Seek : Hidden Dude requires basic system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Enables network data exchange for game updates and ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Checks active connectivity to optimize network requests.
07

Protection of Minors (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Protecting children's online safety is a fundamental priority. Our application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child protection regulations.

We do not knowingly gather personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable legal jurisdictions). If a parent or legal guardian discovers that a child has transmitted personal data, please notify us immediately for swift deletion.

08

Data Deletion & User Rights

In alignment with Google Play Store Data Safety policies, users retain complete rights over their data telemetry:

  • Erasure Requests: You may request the purge of pseudonymous telemetry associated with your Advertising ID by contacting our support team.
  • Local Storage Deletion: Uninstalling the application or clearing App Storage from Android settings immediately removes all locally cached game states.
